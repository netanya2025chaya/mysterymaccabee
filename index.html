RosenDruyan Mystery Maccabee

Welcome to our Mystery Maccabee!

The rules are as follows:

1. Type your name ("Bubby" or "Micah") into the box below and see who you are gifting.
2. Set out on an adventure and spend up to 20₪ buying that person a gift which you think they will enjoy.
3. Wrap the gift so it will remain a mystery.
4. Bring your gift to the RosenDruyan Chanukkah party on the first night of Chanukah.

We shall have a grand unwrapping ceremony together!
Good luck and enjoy!
